CIOS Mission Doctrine
Cyto Intelligence Observation System (CIOS)
Mission

CIOS exists to reconstruct biological reality from temporal microscopy observations.

Its objective is not simply to detect or track cells.

Its objective is to understand the biological story unfolding through time.


-----------------------------------------------
Observation Doctrine
-----------------------------------------------

Observation always precedes interpretation.

Every observation represents evidence.

Evidence gains meaning through temporal context.

No single observation defines biological reality.

Reality emerges through accumulated evidence.

---------------------------------------------
Signal Doctrine
---------------------------------------------

Biological signal must be separated from observational noise.

Signal strength must never be assumed.

Noise must never be interpreted as biology.

Weak evidence remains weak until strengthened by future observations.

-------------------------------------------------
Memory Doctrine
-------------------------------------------------

Snapshots are incomplete.

Temporal memory provides biological context.

Every observation contributes to an evolving understanding of biological identity.

------------------------------------------------
Relationship Doctrine
------------------------------------------------

Relationships are earned through evidence.

Lineage must never be forced.

Identity must never be assumed.

Every accepted relationship must remain biologically explainable.

-----------------------------------------------
Governance Doctrine
-----------------------------------------------

Governance protects biological integrity.

Confidence does not replace evidence.

Uncertainty is preserved whenever biological truth remains unresolved.

Future evidence may strengthen, weaken, or repair previous interpretations.

-----------------------------------------------
Reconstruction Doctrine
-----------------------------------------------

CIOS does not reconstruct images.

CIOS reconstructs biological reality.

The objective is not accurate tracking alone.

The objective is faithful lineage reconstruction through governed observation.

-----------------------------------------------
Final Principle
-----------------------------------------------

Reality.

Carefully Reconstructed.
